Privacy Policy

App: DueMate – Smart Reminder
Developer: Md Nazimuddin
Last updated: November 22, 2026

1. Overview

DueMate Smart Reminder ("the App") is a personal reminder and due-date tracking application for Android. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data the App collects and how it is used.

2. Data Collection

The App does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Specifically:

  • No account or registration is required.
  • No internet connection is required or used.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or tracking SDKs are included.
  • No data is sent to any server or third party.

3. Data Storage

All data you enter in the App (reminder titles, amounts, due dates, categories) is stored locally on your device only using an on-device SQLite database. This data never leaves your device.

4. Notifications

The App uses Android's local notification system to remind you of upcoming due dates. These notifications are generated entirely on your device and do not involve any external service.

5. Permissions

The App requests the following Android permissions:

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS – to display due-date reminder notifications.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM – to schedule reminders at precise times.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED – to reschedule notifications after the device restarts.

None of these permissions are used to collect or transmit personal data.

6. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any data from children under the age of 13.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised date.
